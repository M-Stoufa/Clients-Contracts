Privacy note

Who I am

Mustapha Boussen (Stoufa), freelance developer, Nabeul / Bizerte, Tunisia. Contact: boussenmostafa@gmail.com.

What I collect

Only what you type in the order form: your name, email, optional Discord username, project details, price, deposit, deadline, payment method and signature. Each order also carries an order number, a verification code, timestamps, your timezone and basic device information (browser type), which serve as the record of your agreement.

Why

To handle your order: to reply to you, agree the work, take payment and keep a record of what you agreed to.

Who receives it

The form is sent through Formspree, a US-based form service, which passes it to my email. This means your order details are processed outside Tunisia. By ticking the data consent box on the order form, you agree to this transfer. I do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and I don't share it with anyone beyond what's needed to handle your order.

How long I keep it

For as long as needed to handle the order and to meet my legal and accounting duties. You can ask me to delete it earlier where the law allows.

In your browser

While you fill in the form, a draft is saved in your own browser only, and it is removed when you submit. A submitted order is kept in your browser for up to 7 days so you can change or cancel it. A simple counter of your successful orders is also kept in your browser — after your third order, your direct phone contact appears in the footer. This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Parts of the page load from third parties: fonts from Google, login libraries from Google/Firebase, member counts from Discord — each of them sees a routine visit when your browser fetches those parts.

Accounts (Google or email)

You need an account to submit an order — sign in with Google, or create one with your email and a password (first login creates it). Firebase, Google's login service, handles passwords and sessions; this page never sees your password. Your name and email prefill the form and lock to your account, and your signature must match that name. Email accounts must verify their address (link by email) before submitting. Your profile and order history stay in your own browser only.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing me.
