Quick answers.

What is the minimum order?

10 TND (5 USD / 5 EUR).

How does payment work?

You pay a deposit of at least 20% before I start and the balance before I hand over the final files. I accept D17, Flouci, Payoneer, or crypto.

What about larger projects?

We can split them into milestones with a payment per stage. Mention it in your project description and we'll agree the stages when I confirm the deposit.

Do you work with clients outside Tunisia?

Yes. Price the order in TND, USD, or EUR, and pay with Payoneer or crypto. D17 and Flouci only work if you have a Tunisian account.

How long will it take?

It depends on the project. I confirm a timeline with you after the 15-minute change window.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, for 15 minutes after you sign, right on this page. After that, message me on Discord or by email and mention your order number. Cancelling then follows the cancellation term in the agreement.

Is my order confirmed as soon as I sign?

Signing records your side. After the 15-minute window, I reply in writing to confirm the deposit and accept the order — that reply is what makes it official on my end too.

Can I order more than 2 projects?

One order holds max 2 projects. For 3 or more, message me on Discord or by email and I'll make you a custom bundle quote.

Who owns the finished project?

You do. Your accounts stay yours, and I only get temporary access while I work.

Prefer to talk first?

I usually reply within 24 hours.
